# Recovery V3.14 preregistration: zero-shot Qwen3.6 model holdout on HoVer

Protocol: `recovery-v3.14-zero-shot-qwen36-hover-2026-09-03`

Status: **selection frozen before any Qwen3.6 study-root call; the complete
protocol must be content-addressed before schema smoke or formal calls**.

## 1. Question and frozen claim

V3.14 asks whether the V3.11 group-robust dual-head provenance router transfers
without fitting, threshold selection, prompt adaptation, or label calibration to
an unseen target checkpoint and an unseen fact-verification dataset.

The target is `Qwen3.6-35B-A3B`, served from the local immutable
`Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-FP8` weights. No response from this target was available when
the selection, method, thresholds, gates, and code were chosen. The exact V3.11
relation-head artifact and thresholds are reused:

- provenance-score margin `0.30`;
- relation-confidence margin `0.10`; and
- target-action confidence `0.80`.

The provenance and relation heads decide only whether to execute a candidate
action. Every routed answer is generated by the target model after it sees the
selected evidence. There is no teacher-answer substitution.

Qwen3.6 and one V3.11 development model, Qwen3.5-4B, share the Qwen family.
Therefore a pass supports an unseen-checkpoint, same-family zero-shot transfer;
it does not establish cross-family model generalization.

## 2. Why HoVer, and what was excluded

The remaining FEVER pool cannot support a powered fresh test under the existing
label, root, retrieval-direction, and candidate-order constraints. CLIMATE-FEVER
is not fresh: all 483 eligible items were already evaluated in V3.4.1. V3.14
therefore uses the previously unused local HoVer development release.

HoVer supplies claims, native `SUPPORTED` / `NOT_SUPPORTED` labels, and
multi-hop supporting-fact `(Wikipedia title, sentence index)` pairs. Sentence
contexts come from the official HotpotQA distractor development split because
HoVer records retain the original HotpotQA ID. The frozen local files are:

- `data/hover/hover_dev_release_v1.1.json`; and
- `data/hover/hotpot_dev_distractor_v1.parquet`, SHA-256
  `c20b638ca82b21d04fe12e14ff417ad05153d4d215a65de54497fca4e972f7c6`.

The HotpotQA parquet is the official `hotpotqa/hotpot_qa` distractor validation
artifact. The join is exact on `HoVer.hpqa_id == HotpotQA.id`; a row is eligible
only when every registered supporting-fact title and sentence index exists.

All normalized claims and all component Wikipedia roots are disjoint from the
explicit prior selection manifests frozen in the protocol. This includes the
V3.4.1 CLIMATE-FEVER run, EX-FEVER/FEVER development and formal selections,
V3.11--V3.13 target selections, and the tracked V10--V12 pilot selections.

## 3. Frozen formal sample

The formal set contains 300 items:

- 150 native `SUPPORTED`, represented as `Supported / yes`;
- 150 native `NOT_SUPPORTED`, represented as `NotSupported / no`;
- only two- or three-hop examples;
- 694 distinct annotated Wikipedia component roots; and
- 1,388 distinct auxiliary component roots.

Each item contains three equal-width multi-hop bundles:

1. an anchor bundle drawn from HotpotQA distractor paragraphs;
2. the held-out annotated bundle containing the exact HoVer supporting facts;
3. a retrieval-matched distractor bundle.

Annotated component roots are unique, annotated and auxiliary components are
globally disjoint, and the frozen selection happens to use every auxiliary
component only once (the preregistered maximum is three). Candidate position is
exactly balanced. Within each native label, 75 distractors have lexical score at
least as high as the annotated bundle and 75 score lower. Oriented annotated-role
AUC must be at most `0.65`; the frozen selection value is `0.5841`.

Selection may use labels only for the fixed 150/150 and 75/75 quotas. It cannot
use target responses, router routes, action outcomes, or correctness.

## 4. Immutable execution order

1. Build and audit the 300-example selection.
2. Freeze a V3.14 router manifest that references the V3.11 head byte-for-byte.
3. Encode the 600 formal candidate bundles with Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B in
   inference-only mode. The artifact contains IDs, split names, two provenance
   scores, and two relation vectors only.
4. Content-address code, scripts, data, target weights, selection, router, and
   embedding inputs in the protocol manifest.
5. Verify `/v1/models` and run schema smoke on three old, already exposed V3.7.1
   development examples. Smoke outcomes are never evaluated.
6. Generate five anchor-only actions and three recovery actions for all 300
   formal examples.
7. Freeze primary and matched-budget routes before loading formal labels or
   annotation roles for evaluation.
8. Evaluate once and preserve a negative result if any conjunctive gate fails.

No endpoint substitution, prompt repair, threshold change, sample filtering, or
schema-gate relaxation is permitted after the protocol manifest is written. A
failed endpoint or smoke requires a new version.

## 5. Transport qualification

The target uses `response_format=json_schema`, temperature `0`, at most one
fixed schema-repair attempt, and content-addressed caching. The three-example
smoke contains 24 action rows and qualifies only when:

- all 24 rows are finally valid; and
- at least 23/24 are first-pass valid.

The formal run additionally gates final schema yield at exactly `100%` and
first-pass schema yield at least `95%`. HTTP success alone is not validity.

The deployment is frozen in `scripts/serve_recovery_v3_14_qwen36.sh`: vLLM
`0.28.0`, Transformers `5.16.1`, Torch `2.13.0+cu130`, tensor parallelism over
GPUs 3 and 4, port 31521, 32,768-token context, temperature controlled by the
request, and thinking disabled through the chat-template kwargs. Runtime logs
and PID files are operational state and are not research artifacts.

## 6. Primary policy and baselines

The primary policy is the exact V3.11 dual-head gate. It ranks the two bundles
with Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B, applies the frozen relation head, and routes only when
all of the following are true:

- five-agent anchor agreement is at least `0.80`;
- provenance margin is at least `0.30`;
- relation margin is at least `0.10`;
- the selected target action has confidence at least `0.80`;
- it changes the anchor consensus; and
- it agrees with the frozen relation head.

Registered root-budget-matched baselines are retrieval score, hash random,
fixed candidate 0, fixed candidate 1, and both-candidate acquisition. KEEP is
the no-repair baseline. The available-action oracle is outcome-aware and
diagnostic only.

Retrieval scores and annotation roles are forbidden from the primary router.

## 7. Frozen metrics and conjunctive gates

The unit is the HoVer claim. Native-label-stratified paired bootstrap uses seed
`20261403` and 10,000 replicates. V3.14 passes only if every gate holds:

1. macro-label gain 95% CI lower bound is above zero;
2. zero observed harms;
3. gains for both `Supported` and `NotSupported` are nonnegative;
4. at least ten repairs cite evidence from the annotated bundle;
5. net fixes exceed KEEP and every registered matched-budget baseline;
6. provenance-path accuracy is at least 90%;
7. final structured-output yield is 100%; and
8. first-pass structured-output yield is at least 95%.

The report must also include fixes, harms, route budget, per-label accuracy and
gain, first-pass/final yield, cache hits, all matched baselines, and the
outcome-aware oracle diagnostic.

## 8. Claim boundary

A pass supports zero-target-fit transfer of one frozen provenance-repair gate
to one unseen Qwen checkpoint on HoVer multi-hop evidence. It does not establish
cross-family transfer, universal model transfer, independent publishers, live
retrieval robustness, or financial-market performance. A failure remains a
valid formal negative and cannot be repaired by changing this frozen version.
