# Recovery V3.11 preregistration: group-robust dual-head provenance repair

Protocol version: `recovery-v3.11-group-robust-dual-head-hy-2026-09-03`

Status: **frozen before any Hy study-root call**. A prior request on unrelated
text established only that the endpoint accepts the action JSON schema; it did
not contain a FEVER claim, candidate packet, study root, or correctness label.

## 1. Hypothesis and method

The hypothesis is that cross-model repair fails when a target LLM must both
discover a trustworthy provenance path and self-certify it. V3.11 separates
those responsibilities into two frozen heads and leaves answer execution to
the target model:

1. A frozen `Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B` provenance head ranks the two candidate
   Wikipedia-root packets by claim-to-packet cosine similarity.
2. A logistic relation head, trained on fixed relation embeddings from the 400
   prior 400 V3.7.1 formal roots, estimates whether the selected packet supports or
   refutes the claim.
3. Hy's candidate-specific action is executed only when the provenance margin
   is at least `0.30`, the relation probability is at least `0.10` from 0.5,
   Hy reports confidence at least `0.80`, Hy changes the five-agent consensus,
   and Hy's answer agrees with the relation head.

The thresholds were chosen before Hy study-root calls using outcome-known
Qwen3.5-4B, Ling-3.0-tiny, and SUFE-Fin-R1 records. Five-fold root-held-out
relation predictions were required to produce nonnegative label-group gains
and low damage across all three development deployments. Hy is not used for
head fitting, threshold selection, prompt changes, or calibration.

This is a learned repair policy, not an answer substitution scheme: the final
answer on every routed example is the answer generated by Hy after receiving
the selected evidence packet. The embedding relation prediction is only an
execution gate.

## 2. Data boundary

The formal target contains 188 newly selected FEVER train examples, balanced
94 Supported and 94 Refuted. Every claim and all three Wikipedia roots per
example (anchor and both candidates) must be disjoint from all 1,400 examples
in the V3.7.1 selection. The 188 annotated target roots are mutually distinct
and disjoint from all auxiliary roots. Because only 910 untouched FEVER roots
remain after this exclusion, anchor and unannotated distractor roots may repeat
across formal questions, but no auxiliary root may occur more than 5 times.
Within each native label, exactly half the distractors have a raw retrieval
score at least as high as the annotated packet and half have a lower score.
Candidate order is balanced, and retrieval-score orientation AUC is at most
0.65. These rules are frozen rather than silently weakening the source-root
exclusion boundary.

The target embedding artifact is generated in `--inference-only` mode. It may
contain only example IDs, split names, two provenance scores, and two relation
vectors. It must not contain labels, gold answers, annotation roles, retrieval
vectors, or target-model outcomes.

## 3. Formal execution order

The immutable order is:

1. write and audit the target selection;
2. fit and serialize the relation head and development diagnostics;
3. encode target packets without outcome fields;
4. content-address the complete protocol;
5. run a two-example schema smoke test on old V3.7.1 development roots;
6. regenerate five baselines and three recovery actions for all 188 fresh Hy
   examples;
7. write and hash primary and matched pre-outcome routes using only allowed
   observables; and
8. access gold labels and annotation roles once for evaluation.

No certificate or ledger is generated in V3.11. That removal is the scientific
change under test: the external dual-head gate replaces the target model's
fragile self-issued proof while preserving the V3.7 action prompts.

## 4. Metrics and gates

Question-level, native-label-stratified bootstrap uses seed `20261103` and
10,000 replicates. The result passes only if all gates hold:

- macro label gain 95% CI lower bound is above zero;
- damage among initially correct high-consensus examples is at most 0.5%;
- Supported and Refuted gains are both nonnegative;
- at least 10 repairs cite annotation-supported evidence;
- net fixes exceed KEEP and every budget-matched registered baseline; and
- provenance-path accuracy over all 188 examples is at least 90%.

Registered matched baselines are retrieval score, hash-random candidate,
fixed candidate 0, fixed candidate 1, and both-candidate acquisition. The
available-action oracle is reported only as an offline diagnostic and is not a
primary comparator.

## 5. Claim boundary

A pass supports one-shot transfer of a group-robust dual-head repair gate to an
unseen, separately trained Hy checkpoint on fresh FEVER roots. It does not
establish publisher independence, universal transfer, or novelty relative to
all published literature. The method's literature-level novelty claim requires
a separate up-to-date search before paper submission.
